## Overview

This research examines the impact of the 73rd Constitutional Amendment on Panchayati Raj Institutions over two decades, with attention to decentralised governance, financial devolution, political empowerment and social inclusion.

The paper considers the role of Panchayats in implementing central and state schemes, the progress made in representation and local participation, and persistent constraints including resource limitations, infrastructure gaps, political interference and accountability challenges.
